---
title: Target audience
---

Msc and PhD students with a background in geoscience/engineering but not
geospatial data analysis and/or with a background in ML and modelling, who are
interested in learning about machine learning techniques and their applications
in geospatial contexts. The audience may have varying levels of experience with
programming and statistical analysis, but they should have a basic understanding
of geospatial concepts, data types and statistical methods.
